# Echo Clash Online V0.5.1 Patch

## Loss streak fix
- A Player can gain at most one Loss per scheduled turn.
- Configuration damage and Monster follow-up damage in the same scheduled turn no longer stack multiple Losses.
- The third consecutive Loss triggers exactly one penalty and immediately resets the Loss streak to 0.
- A Loss on the next scheduled turn starts a fresh streak at 1/3.
- Battle Log explicitly records Loss 1/3, 2/3, 3/3 and the reset.

## 3-Loss penalty choices
Players may now choose one of four penalties:
- Return 2 chosen cards.
- Sacrifice Core Vitality: Max HP -100.
- Sacrifice Core Output: Output -1.
- Sacrifice Core Stability: Stability -1.

Echo Clash currently names the casting-capacity stat **Output**.

## AI pacing
- Scheduled AI Actions now wait about 4 seconds before acting.
- AI Counter decisions wait about 1.8 seconds.
- AI virtual d4 rolls pause briefly before resolving.
- The center arena shows an “AI is thinking” notice during an AI scheduled turn.
