1. Goal of the Game

Echo Clash is a 2–8 Player free-for-all. Reduce every opposing Player to 0 HP and become the last living Player. There are no teams in the core mode.

Turn order never rotates. The scheduled order remains P1 → P2 → P3 → … every round. Eliminated Players are skipped.

2. Setup

  • Starting hand: 12 cards.
  • Hand cap: 15 cards.
  • Core Output: 5. Core Stability: 3.
  • Starting HP: 300 for 2–4 Players, 400 for 5–6, 500 for 7–8.
  • Deck scaling: one 108-card gameplay deck per three Players, rounded up.

3. Scheduled Turn

At the beginning of every scheduled turn, that Player automatically draws 1 card, up to the 15-card hand cap. Lingering effects and skipped-turn conditions then resolve before the Player takes their Action.

Automatic Draw 1 does not consume the Action. If you choose Draw as your Action, you draw another +3, meaning a Draw turn can add four cards total.

4. Actions

Each scheduled turn grants one Action. Choose one: cast a Raw Configuration, play one Configured card, summon/replace a Monster, deploy/replace a Barrier, use Support, Draw +3, Trade cards, or Pass.

5. Raw & Configured Cards

A Raw Configuration combines one or more Essence cards, normally one Form, and any number of compatible Forces. Forces multiply damage and stack multiplicatively. Configured cards are prebuilt techniques such as Fireball or Frost Lance.

Barrier Form is exclusive: combine it with Essence cards only. It cannot be combined with another Form or Force.

6. Counter

When Player A attacks Player B, B gets an immediate Counter opportunity inside A’s turn. Counter uses cards but does not consume B’s later scheduled Action.

  • Equal damage: both attacks cancel.
  • Attacker D70 vs Counter D30: D40 remains toward the defender.
  • Counter D90 vs attack D70: D20 travels back toward the attacker.
  • A successful Counter residual that reaches the attacker can earn a Strike.
  • Stunned/Frozen Players cannot Counter.

7. Output, Stability & Rupture

OC compared with statsResult
OC ≤ StabilityStable: 100%, no d4.
Stability < OC ≤ OutputUnstable: roll d4.
OC > OutputRupture: gain Strain and mandatory d4.

d4: 1 = 0%, 2 = 50%, 3 = 100%, 4 = 120%, rounded to the nearest 5. Rupture Strain equals OC above Output. At 2 Strain, a Rupture event triggers D40 self-damage, then consecutive events escalate D80, D160, D320… until the chain resets.

Critical Overreach: OC at least 3 above Output is still legal, but after resolution the caster suffers D300 self-damage.

8. Barrier → Monster → Player

Normal incoming damage travels through the defender’s field in this order: Barrier, then Monster Guard, then Player. Phase Strike bypasses both Barrier and Monster, but Echo Shell can still protect the Player.

Echo Shell: OC3 self-only configuration creating D60 temporary Shell HP. It is not a Barrier and does not use the Barrier slot. Damage hits the Shell before the Player. It disappears when destroyed or at the start of the owner’s next scheduled Action.

A newly summoned Monster does not attack immediately. It attacks after its owner’s next attack opportunity, including a Counter if that occurs first.

9. Area Attacks

An area attack hits the chosen primary target and the nearest living side seats. The attacker is excluded from their own area. Only the primary target may Counter. Side Players resolve their own Barrier → Monster → Player lanes independently.

10. Strikes, Losses & States

A main Player Configuration that reaches an opposing Player grants 1 Strike. Monster attacks do not grant Strikes. Every 5 Strikes grants one Normal State: Vitality +50, Output +1, or Stability +1. Strike overflow is preserved.

Losses are consecutive. At 3 consecutive Losses, sacrifice Core Vitality or return two chosen cards. Successful Player damage / winning a Clash resets the Loss streak. Lingering damage does not count as a Loss.

11. Conditions

Burn, Corrosion and Spike Bind tick at the start of the affected Player’s scheduled turn. Stun/Freeze causes the scheduled Action to be skipped and prevents Counter while active. Cleanse removes supported temporary conditions but not Rupture Strain.

12. Elimination & Tie-Breaker

HP 0 eliminates a Player after the complete resolution chain finishes. Their Monster, Barrier and lingering effects disappear. Last living Player wins.

If every remaining Player reaches 0 during the same resolution chain, tied Players return at 20 HP for a Tie-Breaker Round. They keep their hand, permanent States, Output, Stability and Strike progress. Temporary field effects are cleared. If another tie happens, repeat at 20 HP until someone survives.
